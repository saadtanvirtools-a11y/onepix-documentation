Onepix Shopify Theme
Theme Settings

Logo

The logo is the main brand image displayed throughout your Onepix storefront. Upload your store logo here so it can be used by the theme wherever the logo setting is referenced.

How to Add a Logo

✓

Step 1: From your Shopify Admin, go to Online Store → Themes.

Step 2: Find the Onepix theme and click Customize.

Step 3: In the Theme Editor, open Theme settings.

Step 4: Open Header.

Step 5: Under Logo image, click Select and choose or upload your logo.

Logo Image Setting

In Onepix, the logo option is located inside Theme settings → Header. Use the Logo image selector to choose the image you want to use as your store logo.

Click Select to choose an existing image from Shopify or upload a new logo image. After selecting the logo, save your Theme Editor changes.

Custom Logo Width

Use the Custom logo width setting to control how wide the logo appears in the Onepix theme. Adjust the slider or enter a pixel value in the field beside it until the logo size fits your storefront header.

The screenshot shows the width set to 130 px as an example. Choose the value that best fits your uploaded logo and header layout.
