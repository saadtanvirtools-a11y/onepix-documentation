Product Variants

Use Shopify Admin product variants to create option groups like size, fragrance level, or product type, then manage the generated variant list, prices, inventory, and publishing details.

Open Product Variants

✓

Step 1: Go to Products in Shopify Admin.

Step 2: Open the product you want to edit, or create a new product first.

Step 3: Scroll to the Variants section.

Step 4: Add one or more options such as size, color, fragrance level, or product type.

Create Variant Options

  • Option name: Enter the variant group name, for example Size, Color, or Moisturizer type.
  • Option values: Add the values that belong to that group, such as Medium, Night care, or Fragrance-free.
  • Add another option: Use this when you need multiple option groups for the same product.
  • Done: Confirm the option after entering its values.

Manage the Variant List

After options are added, Shopify generates the product variants and displays them in a variant list table.

  • Group by: Choose how the variant list should be grouped for easier management.
  • Variant: View each generated variant or grouped option.
  • Price: Set or update the price for each variant.
  • Available: Check available inventory across locations.
  • Publishing: View product availability and publishing status.
  • Total inventory: Shopify shows the total inventory across all locations at the bottom.

Best Practices

  • Use clear option names so variant selections are easy for customers to understand.
  • Keep variant values consistent across products when possible.
  • Review generated variants after adding multiple option groups.
  • Update prices, media, and inventory for the variants that need different settings.

Save the Product Changes

✓

Step 1: Review the variant options and generated list.

Step 2: Make any price, inventory, or publishing changes needed for the variants.

Step 3: Click Save.

If your product needs both creation and variants setup, first use Add Product, then configure the Variants section on that product page.
