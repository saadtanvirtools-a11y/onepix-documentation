Onepix Shopify Theme
Store Features

Account Page

The customer Account Page lets shoppers sign in and access their account information from your storefront. In Onepix, the account icon is controlled from the Header settings, while the account experience itself is managed through Shopify customer account settings.

Enable the Account Icon

✓

Step 1: From Shopify Admin, go to Online Store → Themes.

Step 2: Click Customize on the Onepix theme.

Step 3: Open the Header section.

Step 4: Enable the account icon option.

Step 5: Click Save.

Set Up Customer Accounts

Open the Customer accounts settings in Shopify Admin to manage sign-in links, customer account configuration, authentication, self-serve returns, store credit, and the account URL.

  • Show sign-in links: Enable or disable sign-in links in the storefront header and at checkout.
  • Configurations: Customize apps, branding, and supported account features.
  • Authentication: Manage sign-in methods and account access behavior.
  • Store credit: Control whether customers can view and spend available store credit.
  • URL: Use the customer account URL wherever you want shoppers to access their account area.

What This Controls

These Shopify settings manage the actual customer account experience, while the Onepix theme controls whether the account icon is shown in the storefront header.

  • Header account icon: Turn it on from the Onepix Header section in the Theme Editor.
  • Customer account experience: Controlled from Shopify Settings → Customer accounts.
  • Sign-in flow: Customers use the Shopify account system you configure here.

Using the Account Page in Onepix

After enabling the account icon in the header and setting up customer accounts in Shopify, shoppers can click the account icon in your storefront to sign in and access their account area.
