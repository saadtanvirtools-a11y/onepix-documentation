Product FAQ - Metafields

The Product FAQ - Metafields section lets you show product-specific frequently asked questions by connecting the section to the FAQs product metafield. First assign or create FAQ entries on the product in Shopify Admin, then add the Product FAQ (Metafields) section to the required product template in the Theme Editor.

1. Open the Product Metafields

From Shopify Admin, open Products and select the product you want to configure. Scroll down to the Product metafields area near the bottom of the product page.

✓

Step 1: Go to Shopify Admin → Products.

Step 2: Open any product that should display product FAQs.

Step 3: Scroll down until you reach Product metafields.

Step 4: Find the FAQs metafield.

2. Select Existing FAQs or Add a New Entry

Click the FAQs metafield. You can select an existing FAQ entry from the list, or click Add new entry to create your own FAQ content.

  • Select an existing entry: Tick one or more existing FAQ entries that should be connected to this product.
  • Add new entry: Create a new FAQ entry when the required question does not already exist.
  • Per-product control: Because the FAQ entries are assigned through the product metafield, different products can use different FAQs.
After selecting or creating the required FAQ entries, save the product before moving to the Theme Editor.

3. Open a Product Template in the Theme Editor

Next, open the Onepix Theme Editor. Use the template selector at the top and choose Products, then open the product template where you want the FAQ section to appear.

The FAQ metafield section can be added to the product template you are using, such as Default product, product-cart-sticky, product-large-media, product-stacked, products-thumbnails, or thumbnails-2-columns.

4. Add Product FAQ (Metafields)

Inside the selected product template, click Add section. Search or scroll through the section list and select Product FAQ (Metafields).

✓

Step 1: In the selected product template, click Add section.

Step 2: Find Product FAQ (Metafields) in the section list.

Step 3: Click it to add the section to the product template.

Step 4: Save the Theme Editor changes.

5. Edit the Product FAQ Section

After you add Product FAQ (Metafields), the FAQ section appears on the selected product template. Click the section in the left sidebar to edit its appearance and spacing from the right settings panel.

The FAQ questions shown inside the section are pulled from the FAQs metafield assigned to the current product.

  • Heading text: Change the section heading, such as Frequently Asked Questions.
  • Heading size: Choose the display size for the FAQ heading.
  • Heading color: Set the heading text color.
  • Background color: Set the background color of the FAQ section.
  • FAQ questions color: Set the color used for the FAQ question text.
  • Divider color: Set the color of the divider lines between FAQ items.
  • Padding top: Adjust the inner spacing above the FAQ content.
  • Padding bottom: Adjust the inner spacing below the FAQ content.
  • Custom CSS: Add CSS that applies only to this FAQ section.
  • Remove section: Remove the Product FAQ section from the product template if it is no longer required.
The section settings control how the FAQ area looks. To change which questions appear for a product, edit that product's FAQs metafield in Shopify Admin.

How It Works

The section reads the FAQ entries assigned to the current product's FAQs metafield. This allows the same product template to display different FAQ content depending on which product is being viewed.

  • Product-specific FAQs: Assign different FAQ entries to different products.
  • Reusable entries: Existing FAQ entries can be reused across multiple products.
  • Template-based display: Add the FAQ section once to the required product template, then manage the actual FAQ content from each product's metafield.
