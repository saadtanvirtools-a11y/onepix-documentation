Onepix Shopify Theme
Theme Settings

Features

The Features settings control storefront features such as preload, Wishlist, the Wishlist page destination, and quick view.

How to Configure Features

✓

Step 1: From your Shopify Admin, go to Online Store → Themes.

Step 2: Find the Onepix theme and click Customize.

Step 3: In the Theme Editor, open Theme settings.

Step 4: Open Features.

Step 5: Configure the settings shown below and save your changes.

Feature Settings

  • Enable preload: Turn the theme preload feature on or off.
  • Enable Wishlist: Turn Wishlist functionality on or off.
  • Wishlist page: Select the page used for Wishlist.
  • Enable quick view: Turn product quick view on or off.
