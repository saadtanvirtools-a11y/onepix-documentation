Onepix Shopify Theme
Store Features

Add Product

Use Shopify Admin to create a new product, add its product information, organize it for collections and filters, choose a theme template, and save it to your store.

Open the Add Product Screen

✓

Step 1: From Shopify Admin, click Products.

Step 2: Click Add product.

Step 3: The new product screen opens. Add the product details, settings, and media shown below.

Product Information

  • Title: Enter the product name customers will see.
  • Description: Add the product description and formatted content.
  • Media: Upload images, videos, or supported 3D media, or select existing media.
  • Category: Choose the product category when applicable.
  • Price: Enter the selling price and use the additional pricing options when required.

Inventory, Shipping, and Variants

  • Inventory: Turn inventory tracking on when needed and enter quantities for each location.
  • SKU / Barcodes: Add SKU and barcode information when required.
  • Shipping: Set the package, product weight, country of origin, and HS code for physical products.
  • Variants: Add options such as size, color, or other product variants.
  • Purchase options: Configure subscriptions, preorders, try-before-you-buy, or other supported purchase options when used.

Organization and Theme Template

Use the settings on the right side of the Add Product screen to control how the product is organized and displayed.

  • Status: Choose the product status.
  • Publishing: Select the sales channels where the product should be available.
  • Type and Vendor: Organize the product by product type and vendor.
  • Collections: Add the product to one or more collections.
  • Tags: Add tags used for organization and workflows.
  • Theme template: Choose the Onepix product template you want this product to use, such as Default product or another available product template.

Product Metafields

The product screen also includes the product metafields used by the Onepix theme. Fill these fields when you want that information available on the storefront.

  • FAQs
  • Short description
  • Product size
  • How to use
  • Key ingredients
  • Key benefits
  • Skin type

Save the Product

✓

Step 1: Review the product information and settings.

Step 2: Complete the Search engine listing information if required.

Step 3: Click Save.

After saving the product, you can open it again at any time from Products to update its content, metafields, collections, inventory, or assigned theme template.
