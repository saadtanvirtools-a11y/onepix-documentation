Onepix Shopify Theme
Product Pages

Default Product

The Default Product template controls the main product page layout in the Onepix theme. The Product information section manages product media and styling, while the individual blocks inside it control content such as the product title, price, inventory status, variants, quantity selector, buy buttons, custom liquid, and collapsible rows.

How to Edit the Default Product Page

✓

Step 1: From your Shopify Admin, go to Online Store → Themes.

Step 2: Find the Onepix theme and click Customize.

Step 3: From the page selector at the top of the Theme Editor, open Products → Default product.

Step 4: Select Product information from the left sidebar.

Step 5: Configure the main product layout, then click the individual blocks below Product information to edit each product element separately.

Product Information Settings

Select the main Product information section to control the product media layout, colors, spacing, and section-level styling.

  • Size: Choose the product media size, such as Large.
  • Layout: Choose the media layout, such as Horizontal thumbnails.
  • Media right: Move the product media to the right side when enabled.
  • Use custom colors: Enable custom colors for the product information section.
  • Text: Set the product information text color.
  • Background: Set the section background color.
  • Background gradient: Apply a gradient background if required.
  • Top margin: Adjust the outer spacing above the section.
  • Bottom margin: Adjust the outer spacing below the section.
  • Top padding: Adjust the inner top spacing.
  • Bottom padding: Adjust the inner bottom spacing.
  • Theme Settings: Open theme-level options related to this section when available.
  • Custom CSS: Add CSS that applies only to the Product information section.

Product Information Blocks

The Product information section supports multiple blocks. Click Add block to add supported content, or click an existing block in the left sidebar to edit only that part of the product page.

  • Custom liquid: Add custom Liquid content inside the product information area.
  • Title: Edit the product title display settings separately.
  • Price: Manage the product price block separately.
  • Inventory status: Control the stock or inventory-status display.
  • Variant picker: Control how product variants are displayed and selected.
  • Quantity selector: Manage the quantity selector shown before adding a product to cart.
  • Buy buttons: Control the Add to cart and purchase buttons.
  • Collapsible row: Add expandable product information such as description, shipping information, or return policies.
Each block is edited separately. For example, clicking Title opens only the Title block settings without changing the Price, Inventory status, Variant picker, or other blocks.

Editing the Title Block

In your second screenshot, the Title block is selected. Its settings are edited independently from the other product information blocks.

  • Heading size: Choose the display size of the product title, such as Double Extra Large.
  • Top margin: Control the spacing above the title.
  • Bottom margin: Control the spacing below the title.
  • Remove block: Remove the Title block from the product information section.

Additional Product Sections

Below the main Product information section, the Default Product template can also contain additional sections such as Product recommendations, Product FAQ (Metafields), and Blog posts v0. These can be selected separately from the left sidebar when you are ready to document or configure them.
