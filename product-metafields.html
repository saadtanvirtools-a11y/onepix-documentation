Product Metafields

Use Shopify Metafields and metaobjects to create custom product fields such as FAQs, short description, product size, how to use, key ingredients, key benefits, and skin type.

Open Product Metafield Definitions

✓

Step 1: In Shopify Admin, go to Settings.

Step 2: Open Metafields and metaobjects.

Step 3: Click Product to open Product metafield definitions.

Step 4: You will see the definitions list where existing product metafields are shown.

Add a New Definition

From the Product metafield definitions page, click Add definition. This opens the metafield setup screen where you configure the new field.

  • Name: Enter the metafield name, for example Short description.
  • Type: Choose the content type, such as Single line text, Multi-line text, or another suitable field type.
  • Category assignments: Assign the metafield to selected categories when needed.
  • Validation: Use validation rules if the field needs restrictions.
  • Options: Control Admin API, Storefront API, and Analytics related settings when required.

Common Product Metafields

You can create common product metafields like:

  • FAQs
  • Short description
  • Product size
  • How to use
  • Key ingredients
  • Key benefits
  • Skin type

Use Metafields on the Product Page

After saving your definitions, open a product in Shopify Admin. The metafield inputs will appear in the Product metafields area so you can enter values for that specific product.

  • Use the product page to enter values for each metafield.
  • Some metafields can contain multiple values or tags.
  • Text-based metafields such as Short description and How to use can be filled directly from the product editor.
  • FAQ-style metafields can also be managed here after the definition is created.

Save the Metafield Setup

✓

Step 1: Create the required definitions from Metafields and metaobjects → Product.

Step 2: Save the definition form.

Step 3: Open the product and fill in the metafield values.

Step 4: Save the product.

For the Onepix theme, product metafields are useful for content like FAQs, short description, key ingredients, and similar product-specific information.
