Product Large Media

The Product Large Media product template is shown in the Theme Editor as product-large-media. It presents the product with a large media-focused gallery beside the product details. The main Product information section controls the media layout and styling, while the product information blocks can be edited separately.

How to Edit Product Large Media

✓

Step 1: From your Shopify Admin, go to Online Store → Themes.

Step 2: Find the Onepix theme and click Customize.

Step 3: From the product template selector, open product-large-media.

Step 4: Select Product information from the left sidebar.

Step 5: Configure the section settings, then expand Product information and click its individual blocks to edit them separately.

Product Information Settings

Select the main Product information section to control the media layout, colors, spacing, and section-level styling.

  • Size: Choose the product media size. In the screenshot it is set to Large.
  • Layout: Choose the product media layout. In this screenshot it is set to One column.
  • Media right: Move the product media to the right side when enabled.
  • Use custom colors: Enable custom colors for the product information section.
  • Text: Set the product information text color.
  • Background: Set the section background color.
  • Background gradient: Apply a gradient background if needed.
  • Top margin: Adjust the outer spacing above the section.
  • Bottom margin: Adjust the outer spacing below the section.
  • Top padding: Adjust the inner top spacing.
  • Bottom padding: Adjust the inner bottom spacing.
  • Theme Settings: Open related theme-level settings when required.
  • Custom CSS: Add CSS that applies only to this Product information section.

Media Layout

This template emphasizes larger product media. The main image is displayed prominently, with additional product media continuing below the primary image area while the product information remains alongside it.

Edit Product Blocks Separately

The Product information section contains individual product blocks. When the section is expanded in the left sidebar, click any block to edit only that element.

  • Text: Edit supporting product text such as vendor or short information.
  • Title: Edit the product title display settings separately.
  • Price: Manage the price presentation block.
  • Inventory status: Control the stock-status display.
  • Variant picker: Control how product variants are displayed and selected.
  • Quantity selector: Manage the quantity selector.
  • Buy buttons: Control the Add to cart and purchase buttons.
  • Custom liquid: Add custom Liquid content inside the product information area.
  • Collapsible row: Add expandable product information such as description, shipping, returns, or other custom content.
Each block is edited separately. For example, selecting Title changes only the Title block settings, while selecting Price, Inventory status, or another block opens only that block's settings.

Additional Product Sections

Below Product information, the product template can also include additional sections such as Product recommendations and Blog posts v0. These are selected and configured separately from the left sidebar.
