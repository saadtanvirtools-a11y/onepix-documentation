Sections

Featured Product

The Featured Product section lets you highlight a selected product directly on a page. You can show the product image, title, price, variant picker, quantity selector, buy buttons, and share option, while also controlling the section styling and spacing.

How to Add Featured Product

✓

Step 1: In Shopify Admin, go to Online Store → Themes.

Step 2: Click Customize on your Onepix theme.

Step 3: Open the page where you want to show the product.

Step 4: Click Add section and choose Featured product.

Step 5: Select the section to edit its settings and manage its product blocks.

Section Settings

Select the Featured product section itself to edit the overall layout and design options.

  • Layout: Choose the section width, such as Container.
  • Product: Select the product that will be displayed in the section.
  • Media size: Set the product media size, such as Medium.
  • Media right: Enable this option if you want the product media to appear on the right side.
  • Use custom colors: Enable custom colors for this section.
  • Text: Set the text color.
  • Background: Choose the background color.
  • Background gradient: Apply a gradient background if needed.
  • Top margin / Bottom margin: Control outer spacing around the section.
  • Top padding / Bottom padding: Control inner spacing inside the section.
  • Custom ID: Add a custom ID for anchor links or custom targeting.
  • Custom classes: Add custom classes for extra styling.
  • Custom CSS: Add section-specific CSS when needed.

Featured Product Blocks

The section supports editable blocks so you can control what product information appears. In the left sidebar you can manage blocks such as Title, Price, Variant picker, Quantity selector, Buy buttons, and Share.

You can also use Add block to arrange or manage the content blocks inside the Featured Product section.

Title Block Settings

Click the Title block inside Featured Product to edit the product title display.

  • Heading size: Choose the size of the product title.
  • Top margin: Adjust space above the title.
  • Bottom margin: Adjust space below the title.

Price Block Settings

Click the Price block to control the spacing of the price area.

  • Top margin: Adjust space above the price.
  • Bottom margin: Adjust space below the price.
Other blocks such as Variant picker, Quantity selector, Buy buttons, and Share are edited in the same way by selecting the block from the left sidebar.
